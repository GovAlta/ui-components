Feature #1666: Focus and Blur Events

Text Area, Radio, Checkbox, Dropdown, and Date Picker did not support onFocus and onBlur events, unlike Input. This page demonstrates onFocus and onBlur firing for each of those components. For Dropdown, Radio, and Date Picker (composite components), onFocus fires once when focus enters any of their internal fields, and onBlur fires once when focus leaves all of them.
