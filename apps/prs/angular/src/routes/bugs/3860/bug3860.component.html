Bug #3860: App Header Menu aligned to the left instead of the right View on GitHub When the App Header collapses to mobile size, overflowing navigation is collected into a "More" menu (an AppHeaderMenu). Its popover anchors to the LEFT of the button, so when the button sits near the right edge of the screen the menu is squeezed into the remaining right-hand space and appears too narrow. Acceptance criteria: (1) the popover created by AppHeaderMenu can align to the RIGHT of the button so it uses its full width; (2) right alignment only happens when there isn't room to align left. The fix The Popover's automatic right-align compared the space on the trigger's right against the popover's already-squeezed width, so a menu clipped by the screen edge never flipped. It now compares against the width the popover would take with room (its max width), so it right-aligns whenever a left-aligned menu would otherwise be squeezed. How to reproduce Narrow the browser (around 700px, or use responsive/device mode) until several nav items collapse into a More button near the right edge. Open it and watch where the dropdown anchors.
  • Fixed (expected): the menu right-aligns to the button and stays fully on screen.
  • Bug: the menu left-aligns and is squeezed or clipped at the right edge.
Scenario: V2 App Header with overflowing navigation Seven navigation items plus an "Account" menu. At desktop width they sit inline; as the window narrows they overflow into the "More" menu on the right.
